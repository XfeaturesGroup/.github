# Code of conduct

We want participation in XfeaturesGroup projects to be professional,
constructive and welcoming. Treat others with respect, assume good intent and
focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
publishing private information, deliberate disruption, or sustained hostile
conduct. Maintainers may edit, remove or moderate contributions and may limit
participation when needed to protect the community and the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[security@xfeatures.net](mailto:security@xfeatures.net) with the relevant
context. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.
