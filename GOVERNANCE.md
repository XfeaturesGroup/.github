# Governance

Each repository is maintained by the people responsible for its architecture,
operations and release process. Maintainers decide whether to accept changes,
request revisions, schedule releases and retire unsupported work.

For changes that affect public APIs, security posture, data handling or service
operations, maintainers should seek review from the relevant owners before
merging. Urgent incident work may use an expedited path, followed by a written
review of the decision and any follow-up actions.

Repository-specific governance or ownership documents take precedence over this
default.
